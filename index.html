🎬 What is CEB_Ardy?

CEB_Ardy is a free Blender add-on created by Carlos Barreto that brings the power of NVIDIA ARDY – an advanced AI system for generating realistic human movements – directly into your Blender workspace. You don't need to be a programmer or an animator to use it. Just type a description, and watch your character walk, run, jump, or perform custom actions in real time.

It's perfect for game developers, filmmakers, and hobbyists who want professional-quality animations without spending hours keyframing.

✨ Amazing Features

🎤

Text-to-Motion

Type "walk nervously" or "wave hello" – the AI generates the animation instantly.

📍

Waypoint Steering

Place 3D markers in your scene to guide characters along specific paths.

🧍

Pose Constraints

Set custom body positions (like sitting or pointing) and the AI blends them naturally.

👥

Crowd Simulation

Create entire crowds of characters moving together – perfect for city scenes.

🔄

Real-Time Preview

See animations immediately in your viewport – no waiting for renders.

🎚️

Layer & Mix

Combine multiple animations (walk + wave) smoothly for complex movements.

🚀 Getting Started

Follow these simple steps to start animating with AI:

1

Download the add-on: Visit this link to download the application.

2

Open Blender (version 4.4 or newer) on your Windows computer.

3

Install the add-on: Go to Edit → Preferences → Add-ons → Install, then select the downloaded file.

4

Activate it: Tick the checkbox next to "CEB Ardy" in the add-on list.

5

Start creating: Find the CEB Ardy panel in the right sidebar of the 3D Viewport (press N if hidden). Type a prompt or add waypoints, and hit Generate!
